The Rogue 1.0.4by Mag SteelglassBack Story:The Rogue was the first UNS military interstellar vehicle. It had a faster FTL drive than the Apollo 2200, and would get to Ishima 24 years before the Apollo 2200. When the Rogue arrived and heard the news about Earth, they decided to wait for the Apollo 2200 to arrive before making a strike for Earth. In the meantime, they would assist the Ishiman.Description:This plug has 12 new levels, two new weapons, and the number of new ships is debateable.
