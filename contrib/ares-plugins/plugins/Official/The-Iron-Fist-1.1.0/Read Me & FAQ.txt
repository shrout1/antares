																								The Iron FistWhile their cultures have changed, their peoples have had new concerns, and their leaderships have drastically been altered, Gaitor and Obain have always been enemies.After the liberation of Earth, the Cantharan Order started losing it's grip on the Galactic East. And after a successful and bloody rebellion by the Gaitori Isolationists, Gaitor also won it's independence.However, the apple doesn't fall far from the tree, and Gaitor and Obain once again went to war against one another. While most other plugins, including Ares, are no more but 'interactive novels', Iron fist has a branching and deep plot and an interactive storyline with three possible alternate endings. Iron Fist is proudly the first total mission addon for Ares and the first of what will be many quality plugins from Slugsoft Gamestuff.To use The Iron Fist, simply drag the file named The Iron Fist onto the application named Ares and it will start up.Frequently Asked Questions:Q: When I try to start it up, it gives me an endless string of error messages. What's wrong here?A: In order to use The Iron Fist, you need a registered version 1.2.0 of Ares or later.Q: Sometimes Ares quits at random, telling me it was because of an error type 1, 2, or 3.  What's going on?A: Errors 1-3 are memory problems. To fix it, simply increase Memory to Ares.Q: Sometimes Ares quits at random, telling me it was because of an error type 11.  What's going on?A: Error type 11 is a Hardware Exception error. Fill out the bug report form (included) and email it to me at gajet@pacbell.netQ: I complete a level, but when I go back to start a new game, it shows a whole bunch of levels that I haven't played. Why is this?A: Ares wasn't origionally designed to be an interactive storyline, but one can be created by altering victory processes to skip ahead a certain number of levels. However, if you're on a certain level, Ares thinks that you have completed all of the levels before that.Q: You guys are awsome!
