The Audemedon Crisis v1.0.0ßby Martin Mac Devitte-mail: lcanic@eircom.netVersion 1.0.0ßThe Audemedon Crisis is a relatively unsophisticated heavy combat Ares Scenario, built without any programming skills using Hera 1.0.0b6. This demonstrates that even a complete programming illiterate can create something with Hera.Crisis has five relatively simple levels, with seven or more in the planing stages.There are some minor bugs that I cannot seem to solve, but these have no appreciable effect on the operation of the scenario.There are two major bugs that I know of:ONE: I cannot get the “If Player 1 Has No Ships Left declare (Player 0) winner” to work in Chapter Five.
